# Cell lysis and magnetic-bead cleanup

For each of 24 samples, add 80 µL lysis buffer, mix five times, incubate 5 minutes, add 120 µL bead suspension, mix ten times, incubate 5 minutes, place on magnet for 3 minutes, remove supernatant, wash twice with 180 µL wash buffer, air-dry 2 minutes, and elute in 40 µL elution buffer. Do not aspirate beads. Use a fresh tip between samples and a fresh tip for every wash-buffer dispense into a sample well.
